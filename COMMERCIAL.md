# Licensing NovaField

NovaField is dual-licensed.

## Free: GNU AGPL-3.0

Anyone can use, study, change and share NovaField for free under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE): artists, students, studios and companies alike.

The AGPL asks one thing in return: if you change NovaField and give it to others, **or let people
use it over a network** (a hosted service, an internal tool other people log in to, a product built
on it), you must offer them the complete source code of your version under the same license.

Using NovaField as it is, on your own machine, to make your own films, needs nothing more.

## Paid: commercial license

A commercial license lets your company use NovaField **without the AGPL's obligations**. You can:

- build it into a closed-source product, or run it as a hosted or internal service, without
  publishing your changes;
- ship it inside your own software under your own terms;
- get a license grant your legal team can sign off on.

To ask about a commercial license, contact the maintainer through
[github.com/KantLex](https://github.com/KantLex).

## Not covered here

- **The ComfyUI node pack** (`comfyui-novafield/`) is MIT-licensed. See
  [its LICENSE](comfyui-novafield/LICENSE).
- **ComfyUI** and the **models** you run with NovaField have their own licenses. A commercial
  license for NovaField doesn't grant rights to them; check each model's terms before using its
  output commercially.
